# Fixture attribution

Derived from the 10x Genomics dataset *Xenium Prime FFPE Human Ovarian Cancer*
(https://www.10xgenomics.com/datasets/xenium-prime-ffpe-human-ovarian-cancer), licensed CC BY 4.0.
Subset: a 600 µm window, 4 cores of radius 130 µm, 150 genes, the matching H&E crop.
Regenerate with `make fixture` (needs $DATA_ROOT).
